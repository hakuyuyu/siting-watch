## This week's 5 things that matter

1. **Frederick Co., VA has removed data centers from its zoning ordinance, but two grandfathered cases get a final hearing Oct 28.** The Board of Supervisors (BOS) adopted the removal 6-1 on Sep 23. Grandfathering covers only Winchester Gateway (CUP #03-26: 71.85 ac, ~158 MW, per the applicant) and Virginia Technology Park (REZ #04-26: 220.06 ac). Each must be heard by Oct 28, and the Planning Commission recommended denial of both.
2. **Prince William Co., VA has closed its Data Center Opportunity Zone Overlay map (8-0, Sep 22).** By-right data centers now stay only on parcels that were already in the overlay *and* are vested or in the pipeline. The pipeline route needs a legislative or site-plan filing accepted within 90 days, which we compute as about Dec 21, 2026. The same night, the Board denied Dominion's Vint Hill substation SUP 8-0, even though staff recommended approval.
3. **Loudoun Co., VA votes Oct 20 on pausing all legislative data center and substation decisions for up to 12 months**, retroactive to Sep 15. The same agenda includes a motion to move the Golden Substation decision up to **Nov 5**.
4. **Spotsylvania Co., VA's 555-acre Crossroads Technology Campus is next in line for the BOS.** The utility's form lists an estimated load of 894 MW and three new 230/34.5 kV substations. The Planning Commission recommended approval 4-2 on Sep 16, and the BOS hearing date is not yet posted. A draft Comprehensive Plan (recommended 5-0) would cap data center designation at 45% of industrially zoned acreage.
5. **Three moratoria now have known end dates:**
   - Louisville: 180 days, adopted Aug 13. A committee vote on the new data center code is tentatively set for Nov 17.
   - Pima: 120 days from Sep 22, ending about Jan 20, 2027 (computed).
   - DeKalb: to Mar 30, 2027. The definitions ordinance is deferred to a Nov 10 committee meeting.

## Virginia

### Frederick County

**1. Data centers removed from the zoning ordinance, with grandfathering (ADOPTED Sep 23)**
- **Action:** The BOS removed "data centers" as a permitted or conditional use in **all** districts. It also deleted the definition (§165-1105.05) and the use standards (§165-705.01), for "the explicit purpose of prohibiting the development of data centers."
  - **Vote: 6-1** (Oates No). Oates argued for size limits and design standards instead.
  - The Planning Commission (PC) had recommended approval on Sep 2.
- **Grandfathering resolution (7-0):** applies only to **CUP #03-26 Winchester Gateway** and **REZ #04-26 Virginia Technology Park**. Each must be heard "on or before October 28, 2026."
  - If either is approved, it counts as a "significant affirmative governmental act" under Va. Code §15.2-2307.
- **Existing operator affected:** counsel for the operator of the existing data center near Middletown asked for grandfathering of a future expansion. Under the amendment, that facility becomes a non-conforming use.
- **Related item:** the ordinance removing the **TM district** was tabled 7-0 to **Nov 12**, so it can follow the Board's decision on the Virginia Technology Park rezoning.
- **Sources:** [Sep 23 BOS minutes (draft, Oct 14 packet)](https://fcva.granicus.com/services/legistar/download/pdf/4399170/BOS10-14-26MinutesRegMtg260923.pdf) · [Sep 23 agenda](https://fcva.granicus.com/AgendaViewer.php?view_id=1&clip_id=366)

### Prince William County

**6. DPA2026-00006: Data Center Opportunity Zone Overlay District (DCOZOD) amended (ADOPTED Sep 22, 8-0)**
- **What changed:**
  - **No more map additions.** A parcel keeps overlay status only if it was in the overlay the day before adoption **and** meets one vesting/pipeline test:
    - proffers accepted;
    - a preliminary or final plan approved;
    - a zoning determination that is final and no longer appealable; or
    - a data center legislative application, **substation public-facilities review**, final site plan or final plat filed and accepted **within 90 days** (computed: ~Dec 21, 2026).
  - Within the overlay, by-right data centers are allowed in O(L), O(H), O(M), O(F), M-1, M-2 and M/T.
  - Elsewhere in those districts and B-1, a data center needs an **SUP** (FAR increase available by SUP).
  - Data centers are **prohibited** in agricultural, residential, PMR, B-2, B-3 and V districts.
  - Substations need 10-ft opaque fencing facing residential land.
- **Amendment that failed (3-5):** cutting the 90-day window to 0 days. Boddye, Gordy and Stewart voted Yes.
- **Source:** [Sep 22 minutes, Ord. 26-67 (Oct 20 packet Item 4-A)](https://pwcgov.granicus.com/services/legistar/download/pdf/4419009/Item_4-A.pdf)

## Policy trend: tools localities are using

| Tool | Where | Status | What it means for siting |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Remove the use from the zoning ordinance** | Frederick, VA | Adopted Sep 23 (6-1); TM district removal on Nov 12 | No new data center path short of a new text amendment. Only two grandfathered cases remain. Existing sites become non-conforming. |
| **Close the overlay + pipeline window** | Prince William, VA | Adopted Sep 22 (8-0) | By-right status is frozen to vested and pipeline parcels. Everything else needs an SUP, and agricultural/residential districts are barred. Filing window runs to ~Dec 21 (computed). |
| **Pause on board decisions (not a moratorium)** | Loudoun, VA | Vote Oct 20 | Cases still move through the PC, but final votes on data centers and substations wait up to 12 months for Phase 2 rules. Budget for that delay in schedules. |
| **Moratorium (Virginia counsel says it is not legal there)** | Louisville (180 days), Pima (120 days), DeKalb (to Mar 30, 2027) | All in force | DeKalb also freezes permits, LDPs and COs. Pima has a Board waiver path. Each is tied to a code rewrite now in committee. |
| **Caps on size and share** | Louisville (500k sf draft), DeKalb (1M sf draft), Spotsylvania (45% of industrial acreage) | Pending | Hyperscale campuses above these thresholds would need different sites or phased entitlements. |
| **Substation and transmission scrutiny** | Prince William (Vint Hill denied), Loudoun (Golden delays), Frederick (collocation and 500-ft draft policy), Spotsylvania (ROW/underground resolution) | Active | Substation SUPs are now contested on their own, even when staff recommends approval and a utility cites reliability. Expect collocation and setback policies to be written into code. |
| **BESS rules ahead of the state model** | Frederick (Oct 21 hearing), Spotsylvania (Oct 21 briefing), Loudoun (Auto World BESS) | Early | Virginia Dept. of Energy model ordinance expected Dec 1, 2026. Frederick's own advisory committee (DRRC) recommended waiting for it. |
